# Cornell Box

Geometry by Guedis Cardenas and Morgan McGuire, Williams College (2011), from the McGuire Computer Graphics Archive. The original measured scene is from Cornell University. This copy uses the geometry distributed with NVIDIA’s VK Mini Path Tracer. The vertices, normals and faces are unchanged. Redflag assigns diffuse RGB materials to the two colored walls, white geometry and ceiling panel for its rasterized lighting example.
